📖 MONSTERMAYHEM3 — PLAYER GUIDE

The goal is simple: survive as long as you can, defeat evolving monsters, collect gold, and build a stronger arsenal. This guide explains the controls, equipment, upgrades, fusion, and survival rules in plain language.

🎮 1. Controls

Move: WASD or arrow keys. Q: switch between Gun Focus and Melee Focus. Attack: hold left-click or Space. In Gun Focus, aim with your mouse. Pause: Pause button or Escape. New Run: restart a run after confirming.

🔫 2. How combat works

Gun Focus: your gun shoots toward your mouse at full damage. Your melee weapon spins automatically and deals 75% damage when it connects.

Melee Focus: your gun automatically targets the nearest living enemy and deals 75% damage. Your melee weapon is controlled by your attack button and deals full damage.

Each weapon has its own strengths. Guns differ in damage, firing speed, projectile speed, number of bullets per shot, and spread. Melee weapons differ in damage, attack speed, and reach. Explosive weapons can damage enemies near the blast. Your equipped skin can change movement speed, firing speed, and damage.

🪙 3. Gold, score, and healing

Gold is your spending money. Earn it from defeated enemies, gold drops, chests, and events. Walk close to gold drops to collect them. Score rises as you defeat enemies; Best tracks your highest score.

Green healing pickups restore health and also restore your equipped shield. Neither can go above its maximum. A shield absorbs damage before your health; if a hit breaks the shield, leftover damage can still hurt you.

🛒 4. Shop, price ranks, and item status

In each regular shop section, items are listed from lowest price to highest price. Higher-priced items in the same category are balanced to have stronger damage or protection and no worse key performance stats. Guns and melee weapons show damage, firing/attack speed, and approximate damage per second; skins show movement, firing, and damage multipliers; shields show how much damage they can absorb.

Press STATUS to read the full plain-English description and numerical stats, even before buying. An open status panel stays open when the shop refreshes or a message appears. It closes only when you press HIDE STATUS. You can equip one gun, melee weapon, skin, and shield at a time.

Use UNEQUIP to remove an equipped item. Fused items appear in Merged, where you can inspect, equip, unequip, or sell them.

⬆️ 5. Upgrades and refunds

The Upgrades tab improves your equipped gear up to Level 100. Each level costs gold, and later levels cost more. More powerful and expensive equipment also costs more to upgrade: the upgrade price scales with the item's purchase price, with shields and skins carrying their own additional upgrade-cost multipliers. For example, at the same level, an expensive endgame item costs noticeably more to upgrade than starter gear. Selling an item returns its purchase value plus the gold spent upgrading it. Selling removes that item's upgrade progress.

⚗️ 6. Fusion Station

Choose a category and combine two different owned, non-fused items of the same category. The fusion fee is 1,000 gold. Both ingredients are consumed, and a new fused item is created at Level 0. You cannot choose the same item twice or fuse an item that is already fused. Secret Shop items can be used as ingredients.

Before fusing, select both ingredients to see a preview of the new item's name and main stats. Fusion is guaranteed to beat both ingredients at their current upgrade levels in the category’s key stats: guns gain damage, firing speed, and projectile speed, with spread no wider than either ingredient; melee gains damage, attack speed, and reach; skins improve movement, firing speed, and damage; shields gain at least 25% more capacity plus a small bonus.

All gold spent upgrading both ingredients is refunded when fusion succeeds. The 1,000-gold fusion fee is still charged. The new item starts at Level 0, so you can choose how to upgrade it next.

🛡️ 7. Shields and recharging

Your shield takes incoming damage before your health. When it runs out, it starts a 10-second recharge, then refills to its current maximum. Healing pickups can restore a currently equipped shield, and healing never pushes the shield or health above its maximum.

👾 8. Enemies, bosses, and evolution

Enemies have different behaviours: some chase, some attack at close range, and others fire projectiles or use special attacks. Enemies become tougher as the run continues. Every 2.5 minutes, enemy evolution increases enemy strength and gold rewards. Bosses and world bosses are especially dangerous but offer greater rewards.

🌪️ 9. Events and chests

Random events can grant bonus gold, create treasure drops, restore health, or temporarily protect you. Shoot gold chests to open them. Keep moving so enemies have a harder time surrounding you.

🌌 10. Secret Shop

Open the Secret Shop tab and pay 100,000 gold once to unlock it permanently in this browser. Its items are grouped in this exact order: guns → melee weapons → skins → shields. Each group is sorted from lowest price to highest price. Buy items individually; Secret Shop equipment can also be used for fusion.

💀 11. Death and saving

When your health reaches zero, the run ends and your carried gold becomes 0. Your purchased gear, fused items, and upgrade progress remain saved in this browser. Your best score is tracked separately. Choose Play Again to start another run.
