🎮 1. Controls

Move: WASD or arrow keys. Switch focus: Q toggles Gun Focus and Melee Focus. Attack: Hold the left mouse button or Space. Aim: Move the mouse while in Gun Focus. Pause: use the Pause button or Escape. Use New Run to restart the current survival run; confirm before resetting.

🔫 2. Weapon focus and combat

Gun Focus: Your gun fires toward the mouse at full damage while your equipped melee weapon spins automatically and deals 75% damage when its rotating blade connects. You can hold left-click or Space to fire continuously.

Melee Focus: Your gun automatically aims at and fires toward the nearest living enemy at 75% damage. You do not need to aim with the mouse. Hold left-click or Space to spin your melee weapon at full damage. Melee hits depend on weapon reach, the rotating blade's angle, and attack timing.

Weapons have different damage, firing/attack speeds, projectile speeds, pellet counts, spreads, and melee ranges. Upgrading weapons improves their combat performance. Some projectile weapons create explosive area damage.

🪙 3. Gold, score, and drops

Gold is the only spendable currency. Defeat enemies, collect their gold drops, open gold chests by shooting them, and take advantage of random events. Walk close to gold drops to collect them. Green pickups restore health. Score increases as enemies are defeated; your best score is tracked separately.

🛒 4. Equipment shop

Guns: buy and equip ranged weapons. Melee: buy and equip close-combat weapons. Skins: provide different character bonuses. Shields: absorb incoming damage before it reaches health. You keep one gun, one melee weapon, one skin, and one shield equipped at a time. Purchased equipment stays owned between runs unless you sell it.

Fused equipment appears only in the Merged section, not in the regular Guns, Melee, Skins, or Shields lists. Use Merged to equip or sell fused items.

⬆️ 5. Upgrades and full refunds

The Upgrades section upgrades your currently equipped gun, melee weapon, skin, and shield. Each level costs gold and improves that item; the next upgrade costs more than the previous one. Selling an item refunds its full purchase price plus every gold coin spent upgrading it. The upgrade levels are removed when the item is sold.

⚗️ 6. Fusion Station and Merged equipment

At the Fusion Station, choose a category—guns, melee weapons, skins, or shields—and combine two different owned basic items from the same category for a 1,000-gold fusion fee. Both ingredients are consumed and replaced by a unique fused item. You cannot use an item twice in the same recipe, or fuse already-fused or Secret Shop items. Fusion value carries the ingredients' purchase costs and their upgrade investments into the new item's resale value, along with the fusion fee. Fused equipment is listed in Merged, where you can equip it or sell it.

🛡️ 7. Shields and health

Shield points absorb incoming damage before health. If a hit breaks the shield, any leftover damage carries over to health. After a shield is depleted, it begins a 10-second recharge; when the countdown finishes, it refills to its current maximum. Avoid contact damage and enemy projectiles, and collect green healing drops to restore health.

👾 8. Enemies, bosses, and evolution

Enemies have different movement and attack patterns. Ranged enemies fire projectiles, while melee-weapon enemies—including Knife Stabbers, Swordfighters, Axe Maniacs, Hammer Brutes, Spear Guards, Dual-Blade Assassins, Reapers, and the original Blade Stalker—pursue you and attack at close range. Melee-weapon enemies are deliberately more common. Regular enemies grow tougher as score and survival time increase. Every 2.5 minutes, enemy evolution raises enemy strength and gold rewards. Boss versions of enemy types appear during the run, and world bosses are especially dangerous and pay larger rewards.

🌪️ 9. Random events and special objects

Random events may grant bonus gold, create nearby treasure drops, heal you, or temporarily make you invincible. Gold chests appear around the arena; shoot a chest to open it and reveal a gold reward. Keep moving so enemies cannot surround you.

🌌 10. Secret Shop

Pay 100,000 gold once to permanently unlock the Secret Shop in this browser. Its high-cost cosmic weapons are purchased with gold. Unlocking the shop is separate from buying its items.

💀 11. Death, saving, and survival

When health reaches zero, the run ends and you keep half of your current gold, rounded down. Your purchased arsenal, merged equipment, and upgrade progress persist. The game autosaves in this browser, so reopening it in the same browser profile can restore your progress. Best score is tracked separately. Use Play Again to begin another run.
