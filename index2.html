📖 HOW TO PLAY

Movement: WASD or arrow keys. Aim: Mouse. Shoot: Left-click or Space. Attack: Hold left-click or Space. Switch weapon focus: Q.

Loadout: You always have one gun and one melee weapon equipped. In gun focus, shoot manually while melee spins automatically at reduced damage. In melee focus, the gun auto-fires at reduced damage; hold left-click or Space to spin the melee weapon for full damage.

Shields: A shield absorbs damage before your health. If one hit breaks it, any excess damage carries over to your health. A depleted shield fully recharges after 10 seconds.

Enemies: Different enemy types have different behavior. Enemies become tougher as the run progresses and as your equipped gear gets stronger. Their gold rewards are based on enemy type, not their scaled stats.

Bosses: Each regular enemy type has a matching boss variant. Bosses appear periodically and pay more gold. Defeat four bosses or reach 10,000 score to unlock the Secret Shop.

Survive: Collect green healing drops, shoot gold chests to open them, save your run, and try to beat your high score.
